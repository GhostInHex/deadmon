Provenance

Read this before you judge the numbers.

The cost figures above are measured from this repo. They come from running the pipeline and reading the run history, and they are the numbers I can defend.

The tier prices are not set. They are shown as “—” because choosing them is a business decision, not a rendering one. Nothing on this page has been produced by cfo.ai — Ari was not available to generate it, and inventing pricing or revenue would misrepresent the work.

Runway is therefore not stated. Runway needs a price, a conversion assumption and a cost-per-user figure, none of which exist yet. See docs/business-plan.md once those are supplied.
