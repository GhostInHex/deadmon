FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Who can participate?
Our public hackathons are open to developers, researchers, and builders worldwide — from students to senior engineers.
Is it free to join?
Yes. Joining the community and participating in public hackathons is free.
Do I need a team, or can I join solo?
Both work. Register solo and form or join a team on the platform before the event starts.
What can I win?
Prizes are announced with each event — alongside mentorship, partner tools and credits, and exposure to our sponsor network.
Is it online, in-person, or hybrid?
Each hackathon sets its own format — online, in-person, or hybrid — and the event page always states it up front.
How is judging done?
Submissions are reviewed by expert judges against the published criteria for each event.
How can my company sponsor a hackathon?
Use the Become a sponsor form — tell us about your organisation and we'll get back within 2 business days.
Where can I see past winning projects?
Browse the Previous Hackathons section above — winner showcases are published after each event.
